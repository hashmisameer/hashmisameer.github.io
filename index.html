FAQ

Common questions

For anything not covered here, send your question on WhatsApp.

Where is our data stored?

On your own server, inside your premises. Nothing is stored on an external cloud unless you request it, and every user receives only the access their role requires.

Do we need to replace Tally or our ERP?

No. These systems cover the processes your existing software does not, such as purchase follow-ups, documentation and renewals. Your current software continues as it is.

Can installation be done remotely?

Yes. Installation and training are carried out through screen-sharing sessions that your team approves and observes.

How is pricing structured?

A fixed build price per system, a one-time setup fee, and a quarterly maintenance plan covering all your systems. Any new feature is quoted and approved before work begins.

How is support handled after go-live?

Bug fixes, backups and minor changes are covered by the maintenance plan, with direct access by phone and WhatsApp.
